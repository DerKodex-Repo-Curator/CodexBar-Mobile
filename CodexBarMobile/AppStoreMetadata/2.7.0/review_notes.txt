CodexBar is an iPhone/iPad companion for the Mac app. No separate account login is required. For review without a Mac, dismiss the initial Release Notes with Done, then tap View Demo on the Usage tab while it shows Waiting for Mac Sync to explore synthetic provider usage and costs. Live data requires the Mac companion on the same iCloud account. Mac download: https://github.com/o1xhack/CodexBar-Mobile/releases . Version 2.7 adds a Provider Selection option to the CodexBar widget's Overview: By Weekly Reset (Soonest First) shows 2, 3, or 4 providers ordered by their next weekly reset, calculated on the device from data already synced; Choose Manually keeps the previous behavior. It also recognizes Grok's weekly limit in the Usage card order. This release is iOS-only; the paired Mac version remains 0.73.0.1 Mobile 2.6.0.
