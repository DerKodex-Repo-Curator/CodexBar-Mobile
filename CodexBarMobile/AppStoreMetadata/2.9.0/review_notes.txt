CodexBar is an iPhone/iPad companion for the Mac app. No separate account login is required. For review without a Mac, dismiss the initial Release Notes with Done, then tap View Demo on the Usage tab while it shows Waiting for Mac Sync to explore synthetic provider usage and costs. Live data requires the Mac companion on the same iCloud account. Mac download: https://github.com/o1xhack/CodexBar-Mobile/releases . Version 2.9 displays four more providers synced from the Mac (Claude Platform, fal, FLORA, Parallel), the Claude Platform Console credit balance with per-grant remaining amounts and expiry days, more translated provider details, Claude accounts grouped by the Mac's sign-in email, and exact costs for ChatGPT-created Codex Auto-review requests. All data comes from the user's own Mac through their private iCloud database; the iPhone makes no provider network requests. The paired Mac version is 0.76.0.1 Mobile 2.9.0.
